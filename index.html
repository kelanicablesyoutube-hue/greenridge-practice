About us

Three generations of growing in the Wayamba region

Greenridge started as a small family coconut estate. Today we farm coconut, cinnamon, pepper and ornamental plants, and we pack and ship them to buyers in Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

The same team that cares for our estates now builds gardens, so every landscape uses plants we know will thrive in Sri Lanka's climate.

  • Own estates, so quality is controlled from soil to shipment
  • Export-grade grading, packing and documentation
  • Phytosanitary certificates arranged for every order
  • Local nursery stock for landscaping projects
